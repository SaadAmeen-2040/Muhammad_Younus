Questions

Frequently asked questions

Are hosting and domain included?

No. Hosting is where your website files live, and a domain is your web address. You pay the provider directly, and the accounts stay yours. I can guide you to buy and connect them.

How long does it take?

5 to 7 days. It starts when I receive your first payment and the content I need from you.

What is a revision?

One round of changes after you see the website. 3 rounds are included. Sending all changes in one list makes each round faster.

Is my website secure?

Every package includes HTTPS, the padlock in the browser, so browsers do not show a "Not secure" warning.

Will my website be number one on Google?

No one can honestly promise that. SEO and Google setup prepare your website so Google can find it. Results depend on your business, your area, and competition.

What happens after delivery?

Premium includes 1 month of support for bug fixes and content changes. After that, or for other packages, you can choose a monthly maintenance plan for updates, backups, and small changes.

Who owns the website?

You do. After the final payment, the website belongs to you.

Do you build online shops or apps?

They are not part of these packages. Tell me what you need and I will explain what is possible and give a separate price.
